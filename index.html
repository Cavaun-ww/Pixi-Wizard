Questions

Good to know

Do people need to download an app?

No. They open your link or scan your QR code, tap "Start AR camera", and point their phone at the photo. It works right in the phone's browser.

What can I put on top of my photo?

A video (MP4 or WebM), an animated GIF, or another photo. Videos up to 20 MB, and if yours is bigger, we'll help you trim it.

What kind of photos work best?

Photos with lots of detail and contrast are easiest for the camera to recognize, like posters, artwork and busy pictures. Plain logos or mostly blank images are harder.

What happens if I cancel Pro?

Everything you've made stays live on your page. You just can't make new ones until you resubscribe.
