Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Pixi Wizard ("we", "us") lets people upload a photo plus a video, GIF or photo and share them as an augmented reality (AR) experience. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Account info: your email address, and your name and profile picture if you sign in with Google.
  • Profile info you add: your page link (handle), display name and bio.
  • Content you upload: photos, videos and the titles you give them.
  • Subscription info: if you subscribe, PayPal handles your payment. We receive your PayPal subscription ID, status and the email on your PayPal account. We never see or store your card or bank details.

What's public

Your profile page, your handle, display name, bio, and every AR experience you publish (photo, video and title) can be seen by anyone with the link. Don't upload anything you want to keep private.

Camera

When you view an AR experience, your phone's camera is used live in your browser to find the photo. Camera images are never recorded or sent to us.

How we use your info

  • To run your account and show your AR experiences and profile page.
  • To manage your subscription.
  • To keep the service safe and remove content that breaks our Terms.

We don't sell your personal information and we don't use it for advertising.

Services we use

  • Supabase stores accounts, uploads and profile data.
  • PayPal processes subscription payments.
  • Google provides "Continue with Google" sign-in, if you choose it.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website.

Deleting your data

You can delete any AR experience from your page at any time, which removes its photo and video. To delete your whole account, contact us through the email listed on our site and we'll remove it.

Children

This service isn't intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions? Reach us through the email listed on our site.
